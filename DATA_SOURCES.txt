OpenStreetMap contributors, distributed by Geofabrik
Dated extract: https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/belgium-260801-free.shp.zip
Copyright and licence: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright (ODbL).
Derived data: occupancy rasters, road reference points and polygon-ray geometry.
The original submission's exact extract date was not recorded. Its processed
caches are archived; all new geometry checks identify the pinned dated extract.
Retain OpenStreetMap attribution and applicable ODbL terms for derived databases.

raw/manifest.json records exact source URLs, sizes and SHA256 hashes for the
revision downloads. code/download_data.py checks these pinned hashes.
Raw archives are downloaded separately and omitted from the GitHub package.
